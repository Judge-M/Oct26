# Team coaching

Coach general-purpose teams on their currently owned IRIS ticket. Questions,
source references, graduated hints and answer walkthroughs are authored in
expanded/author.py and generated into expanded/tickets.json for preparation.
Keep controller answers outside the participant evidence share.

Help teams locate the evidence, navigate the named tool and repeat the relevant
observation. Then ask what that observation establishes and what remains unknown.
Use free hints at any time; do not impose timed waits, penalties or approvals.

Useful prompts include: Which clock produced this timestamp? Does the hash match?
Does the record describe a request or a completed transfer? Does a password reset
invalidate this session? What systems have missing collection coverage?

Teams submit answers through CTFd. The integration publishes predefined findings
to IRIS and preserves progress when ownership changes. Do not ask teams to write
initial reports or a joint assessment as a condition of completion.

See [participant guidance](../expanded/guides.md) and
[controller operations](../docs/expanded-operations.md).

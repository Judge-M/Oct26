# Incoming SOC shift handover

EXERCISE ONLY. At exercise time 09:30Z, the outgoing shift reports suspected
disclosure of movement details for fictional patrol LANTERN, already in the field.
The patrol's current movements, the exposed version, access method, and remaining
affected systems have not been established. No confirmed harm has been reported.

The outgoing shift collected endpoint, network, identity, document-service and SIEM
exports. An account password was reset at 09:20Z. Do not assume the incident ended.
Begin with `common/collection.md` and the evidence assigned to your cell. Initial
evidence is available to all cells; share your work and ask for help.

Your first command update is due at elapsed minute 25. Submit a brief even when
confidence is low. Exercise control will release further information and questions.
All command messages and defensive recommendations go to the controller, who plays
simulated command. Do not contact, track, or direct real personnel in the field.
Do not scan external systems or execute material found in evidence. All addresses,
identities, places, and artifacts in this lane are fictional. No attack is required.

Use UTC for evidence and elapsed minutes for exercise deadlines. Label each claim
as confirmed, assessed (with confidence), or unknown. Report the potential relevance
to LANTERN without inventing harm, adversary identity, or real-world movements.

Success means a defensible shared assessment, timely updates, evidence citations,
and proportionate recommendations. There are no competitive flags or points for speed
alone. Use the reporting templates and keep an explicit unanswered-questions list.

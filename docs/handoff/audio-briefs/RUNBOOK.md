# Narrative delivery runbook

## Before participants arrive

1. Confirm the only active scripts are the two files under `spoken/`.
2. Run `python docs/handoff/audio-briefs/validate.py`.
3. Confirm no WAV, MP3, OGG, voice-rendering scripts or superseded brief directories are present in this package.
4. If final audio or video has been accepted separately, load it on the facilitator's presentation machine and test the room output. Do not publish its URL to participants. Otherwise plan to read the conclusion live.
5. Open [player.html](player.html) on the facilitator machine.

## Cue 01 — introduction

Deliver [brief-01-introduction.txt](spoken/brief-01-introduction.txt) after teams assemble and before investigative work begins. The cue does not start the event clock and does not release evidence.

## Cue 02 — final assessment

Do not deliver the conclusion until all of the following are true:

- Shared progress displays `20 of 20 ticket stages complete`.
- The participant page displays `Assessment complete — LIMITED DISCLOSURE CONFIRMED`.
- The full final-assessment panel and containment actions are visible.
- IRIS has accepted the final assessment publication.

Then deliver [brief-02-exit.txt](spoken/brief-02-exit.txt) or play separately accepted matching audio. The script restates the published conclusion; it must never be used to help participants reach that conclusion.

If the final assessment is not visible, do not improvise or use a generic handover. Diagnose the completion/publication problem and retain the facilitator’s in-person administrative close separately.

## After delivery

Preserve the case record and continue with the separate in-person administrative brief and AAR. The narrative conclusion does not replace safety, logistics, scoring or exercise-administration remarks.

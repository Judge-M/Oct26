# Live facilitation boundary

The two Faye Maxwell scripts are the only approved prerecorded narrative cues.

Facilitators may still coach teams in person, ask them to cite evidence, challenge unsupported conclusions and help them distinguish confirmed, unproven and unresolved findings. That live coaching is not another narrative brief and must not disclose scored answers.

Do not create or reuse midpoint, post-lunch, evidence-inject or generic stand-down recordings. The participant application handles evidence progression and publishes the final assessment automatically when completion conditions are met.

After the conclusion, conduct the separate in-person administrative brief and AAR using `facilitator/assessment-aar.md`.

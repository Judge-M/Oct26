# Optional live analytical exchanges

These coaching exchanges are separate from the two recordings. Use the existing agenda allowance, rotate spokespeople and challenger cells, and keep investigation and AAR time distinct. They add no scores, report submission, approval requirement or ticket unlock. Do not hold evidence for a discussion. Reconcile timing with the issued agenda rather than treating planning estimates as measured pacing.

| Exchange | When usable | Timebox |
|---|---|---|
| Interim assessment | Issued update seam; current evidence only | 60 s assessment, 20 s challenge, 20 s response, 20 s feedback |
| Evidence revision | Once a cell has analyzed genuinely available new evidence | 30 s before/evidence/after, 10 s challenge, 10 s response, 10 s feedback |
| Recommendation | Before the exit, using the current assessment even if incomplete | 60 s recommendation/alternative/tradeoff, 20 s challenge, 20 s response, 20 s feedback |

Prompts (live only):

- **Assessment:** “Give me your current assessment and confidence, the source behind it, and the uncertainty that matters most. What is your next useful investigative step?”
- **Revision:** “What did you assess before? What does the new evidence support now? Explain what changed in your claim or confidence, and what you still cannot establish.”
- **Recommendation:** “Give me a proportionate containment recommendation and one alternative. Explain your evidence, the operational or evidence-preservation tradeoff, and what would change your mind. These are recommendations; do not carry them out.”

Another cell challenges a claim with a source or a question about its limits. The speaker defends or revises the assessment. Give specific feedback on reasoning: a source cited, a justified uncertainty, or a revision that follows from evidence. Do not provide a scored value, settle attribution or fill an intentional collection gap.

If there is no analyzed new evidence, defer the revision exchange to AAR. If recommendations are incomplete, say what can be supported and what further collection is needed. Do not wait indefinitely for T20 or announce its release to justify a discussion. At the closing seam, preserve existing case work and stop investigation. The exit handover adds no separate report or grading gate.

The three optional exchanges total five minutes. The introduction and exit are about 203 seconds combined before pauses and belong at their actual seams. Rehearse the complete agenda and measure pacing. For any deliberate clock pause/resume, use the event runbook and verify the actual mode. Keep performance observations, names and feedback private. In AAR use facilitator/assessment-aar.md to turn findings and remaining gaps into concrete improvements.

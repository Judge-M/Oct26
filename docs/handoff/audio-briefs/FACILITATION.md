# Participant briefings and decision practice

Facilitator-only. Use alongside [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md). These three live activities turn the dispatches into participant explanation, peer challenge and feedback. Keep the seven brief scripts and their exact text echoes unchanged.

Participants are expected to take part as coaching. These activities award no points, impose no penalties and require no new report submission. Activity completion is never a prerequisite for claiming, answering, evidence release or ticket completion. Normal paused-mode behavior still applies during the timed facilitated windows. The [existing scoring rules](../../../facilitator/assessment-aar.md) remain authoritative. Recommend simulated actions to the controller; do not execute containment, change evidence or direct the patrol.

## Preparation and time budget

- Confirm the first-update deadline on the issued schedule before opening. If the schedule lacks it, the organizer must set and communicate that deadline during orientation; do not leave a template placeholder or improvise an inject time.
- Identify two different cells for each exchange: a speaking cell and a peer challenger. Choose from actual current work and shared findings, not the rehearsal's historical team assignments. Rotate cells and spokespeople across the three activities; use the AAR to hear from cells that have not spoken. Never wait for a particular cell to finish a ticket.
- Ask every cell to prepare its own short assessment in its existing notes. During another cell's turn, compare its claim with your evidence and prepare one challenge or uncertainty. No extra form or submission is required. Invite a different challenger if the named cell is unavailable.
- Redistribute the schedule model's existing ten-minute facilitated allowance: activity A takes two minutes, B one minute, and C two minutes. Run one of each, not B after every inject. Four inject dispatches plus Brief 06 add about 175 seconds of speech, bringing these exchanges and dispatches to about 7 minutes 55 seconds. Leave the remaining roughly two minutes for pauses, sound cues and transitions. Opening tasking belongs to orientation; ENDEX belongs at the AAR seam. Do not add another ten-minute recap on top of this allowance.
- These are facilitation budgets, not measured human pacing. Time the whole sequence in rehearsal. If it overruns, reduce extra discussion or carry it into the reserved AAR; do not extend the agenda silently or rush evidence publication.
- For each live exchange, the organizer pauses elapsed investigation time using the existing event runbook and verifies paused mode, then resumes through the normal readiness gates and verifies running mode at the end of the timebox, even if the briefing is incomplete. Short audio dispatches retain their existing clock behavior. The page and the spoken prompt cannot pause the controller. Keep a technical failure separate from the coaching timer.

## A — Interim command update: two minutes

**Trigger:** the first-update deadline in the issued schedule, whether or not any inject has arrived. Preliminary findings and low confidence are acceptable. This is the first of two active cross-cell briefings; C is the second.

**Before the exchange:** select a cell with an evidence-backed observation and a challenger from another cell. If no cell has a supported finding yet, ask for an observation, uncertainty and next investigative step; do not supply a missing answer.

**Read aloud:**

> Incoming watch, give command a sixty-second update. State your current assessment and confidence, cite the evidence, name the most important uncertainty, and recommend the next defensive or investigative step. Another cell will challenge one claim. Use only evidence currently available to you.

| Time | Who | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 60 s | Speaking cell | Assessment, evidence citation, uncertainty and recommended next step. |
| 20 s | Peer cell | Challenge one inference, missing source or scope boundary. |
| 20 s | Speaking cell | Defend, narrow or revise the claim. Saying the evidence is insufficient is acceptable. |
| 20 s | Facilitator | Give one specific piece of feedback using the guidance below. |

**Response scaffold:** “We assess ___ with ___ confidence because ___. We cannot yet establish ___. We recommend ___ next because ___.” The scaffold is not an answer key and must not be filled in by EXCON.

## B — Evidence revision check: one minute

**Trigger:** after a cell has investigated one verified new release and can compare it with an earlier assessment. Choose a meaningful revision or a reasoned decision to retain the claim. Release alone does not mean analysis is complete. This may follow any of Briefs 02–05; their numbering is not a sequence requirement.

If evidence arrives before activity A, B may occur first. Run B only once during the event. If no cell is ready before the closing seam, defer this reflection to the AAR and record why; do not force an invented change or delay releases.

**Read aloud:**

> What did you assess before this evidence arrived? What does the new evidence support now? Explain what changed in the claim or confidence, and what still cannot be established. If your conclusion stayed the same, explain why.

| Time | Who | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 30 s | Speaking cell | Before, new evidence, after, and remaining limitation. |
| 10 s | Peer cell | Ask which cited observation justifies the change or retention. |
| 10 s | Speaking cell | Answer or narrow the claim. |
| 10 s | Facilitator | Recognize the evidence-based revision or justified restraint. |

Use the actual earlier note or shared finding where available. Do not overwrite a published finding, edit an answer, or invent a previous belief to manufacture drama. Record a changed assessment in the cell's normal notes; accepted answers continue to publish through the existing platform workflow.

## C — Containment recommendation and challenge: two minutes

**Trigger:** after verified T20 availability and delivery of Brief 06. Prepare the exchange from currently supported shared findings; T20 availability does not establish that every ticket is complete. Use a speaking cell and peer cell that differ from A where practical.

**Read aloud:**

> Give command one proportionate containment recommendation and one alternative. Explain the evidence supporting your choice, the expected benefit, the disruption or evidence-preservation tradeoff, and what additional evidence would change your recommendation. A second cell will challenge the choice. These are recommendations to simulated command; do not carry out the actions.

| Time | Who | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 60 s | Speaking cell | Recommendation, alternative, supporting evidence, tradeoff and decision-changing information. |
| 20 s | Peer cell | Challenge proportionality, collection limits, preservation or the alternative. |
| 20 s | Speaking cell | Defend or revise the recommendation; state remaining uncertainty. |
| 20 s | Facilitator | Give specific feedback and one question to carry into the AAR. |

**Response scaffold:** “We recommend ___ rather than the alternative ___ because the evidence supports ___. The benefit is ___; the cost or preservation risk is ___. We would revise this recommendation if ___.” Participants supply the options. EXCON must not offer the scored remediation answer as a menu item or confirm it before discovery.

**Closing fallback:** if T20 remains locked, omit Brief 06. At the organizer's closing assessment seam before ENDEX, use this same generic recommendation exercise with currently released evidence and explicitly acknowledge that synthesis is incomplete. Do not claim T20 unlocked, unlock it manually, or require a conclusion about unreleased material. If the hard stop leaves no time, defer C to discussion of preserved findings in the AAR, without reopening investigation, and note the missed live exchange. ENDEX is not delayed by an unmet ticket prerequisite or an incomplete briefing.

## Feedback without supplying the investigation

Use one observation about the participants' reasoning, followed by one next question. Check their cited source against released evidence and, where applicable, already published shared findings. If you cannot verify the citation in the window, say verification is pending and return to it in the AAR; do not give an unearned confirmation.

- **Evidence:** “You cited the source and separated its observation from your inference. What independent evidence would strengthen that inference?”
- **Revision:** “You explained why your confidence changed. Which part of the earlier assessment still holds?”
- **Restraint:** “You kept that conclusion within the available collection. That restraint is useful. What collection would let you make a stronger claim?”
- **Overreach:** “Your source supports a narrower statement. Can you restate the claim within what it actually records?”
- **Decision:** “You identified both a benefit and a preservation cost. What would make the alternative preferable?”

Praise reasoning and justified uncertainty explicitly, not confidence, speed, a particular host verdict or agreement with EXCON. Do not resolve intentional gaps, add a new adversary or consequence, introduce unreleased facts, grade the briefing, change scores, or make operational decisions for the patrol. Feedback is coaching, not a new authored finding or evidence inject.

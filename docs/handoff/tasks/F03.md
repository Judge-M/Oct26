## Acceptance checks

Proposed starting targets: p95 ordinary app requests under 2 s, normal score/finding delivery under 10 s, no OOM/swap collapse and at least 20% measured capacity reserve; revise targets explicitly before the run. Unsupported hosts fail readiness.
